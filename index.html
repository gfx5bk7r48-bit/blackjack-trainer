How to use

Tap 🎤 Start voice and allow the microphone. Then say:
• my card queen, my cards eight six: your cards
• dealer card six / dealer six: dealer's upcard (more dealer cards count but don't change advice)
• seven, king ace four: other players' cards (count only)
• One sentence can mix them: my card jack five dealer nine. Say other to switch back to other players' cards in the same breath.
• new hand (count kept) · new shoe / shuffle (count reset) · undo
• split splits your pair; next or stand moves to your next split hand.
Count: Hi-Lo (2–6 = +1, 7–9 = 0, 10/J/Q/K/A = −1). True count = running ÷ decks left, rounded down for bets and index plays.
If the mic misbehaves, use the buttons: pick Mine / Dealer / Other, then tap cards.
